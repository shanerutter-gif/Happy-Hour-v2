Spotd for Business – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from venue owners who use Spotd for Business, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you claim or manage a business listing, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and login credentials for the business portal.
  • Listing content: your venue name, address, hours, happy hour details, events, photos, and any other information you add to your listing.
  • Usage information: basic records of how you use the business portal, such as when you update your listing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the business directory, including displaying your listing to guests.
  • Manage your business portal account and authenticate your access.
  • Communicate with you about your listing, updates, and support requests.
  • Improve Spotd for Business and develop new features.

3. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Data

Spotd does not sell your personal information to third parties. Your listing content (venue name, hours, events, photos) is displayed publicly as part of the directory — that is the purpose of the service. We do not sell your account information.

4. Sharing

We may share information with service providers who help us operate Spotd (such as hosting providers), subject to confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Spotd, our users, or others.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You can update your listing and account information through the business portal at any time. To request deletion of your business portal account, contact us at shane@spotd.biz.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a revised date.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at shane@spotd.biz.
